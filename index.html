Overview

The Chinese Revolution refers to the major political transformations in China during the 20th century. The 1911 Xinhai Revolution ended centuries of imperial rule (Source 2), while the Communist victory in 1949 established the People’s Republic of China (Source 1).

Timeline

  • 1894–1911 — Revolutionary movements grow before Xinhai (Source 2)
  • 1911 — Qing Dynasty ends (Source 2)
  • 1927–1937 — KMT vs CCP conflicts (Source 9)
  • 1937–1945 — Japanese invasion (Source 1)
  • 1946–1949 — Civil war → PRC founded (Source 1)

Causes

  • Weakening of Qing rule (Source 2)
  • Nationalism & new political ideas (Source 10)
  • KMT & CCP political conflict (Source 9)
  • Impact of Japanese invasion (Source 1)
