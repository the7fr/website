Overview

The term Chinese Revolution can refer to two major events:

  • 1911 Xinhai Revolution — overthrew the Qing dynasty and established the Republic of China.
    (Source: Columbia University Asia for Educators — .edu)
  • Chinese Communist Revolution (1945–1949) — the Chinese Communist Party defeated the Kuomintang and founded the People’s Republic of China on October 1, 1949.
    (Verified by Stanford/SPICE + U.S. State Dept.)

Causes (Short Summary)

  • Qing dynasty weakened by foreign invasions and internal rebellions
  • Rise of nationalism and Sun Yat-sen’s ideas (Three People’s Principles)
  • Conflict between the Kuomintang (KMT) and Chinese Communist Party (CCP)
  • Warlord era + Japanese invasion weakened national unity

Timeline

  1. 1894–1911: Sun Yat-sen organizes revolutionary groups in exile.
  2. 1911: Wuchang Uprising → Qing dynasty collapses → Republic of China (1912).
  3. 1927–1937: KMT–CCP conflict; First United Front collapses.
  4. 1937–1945: KMT & CCP unite against Japan during WWII.
  5. 1946–1949: Chinese Civil War resumes → CCP victory.
  6. Oct 1, 1949: Mao Zedong proclaims the People’s Republic of China in Beijing.
