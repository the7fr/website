Overview

The Chinese Revolution includes two major turning points in Chinese history:

  • 1911 Xinhai Revolution: Ended the Qing dynasty, forming the Republic of China.
  • 1945–1949 Communist Revolution: Established the People's Republic of China under Mao Zedong.

Timeline

  1. 1911 — Wuchang Uprising
  2. 1912 — Republic of China founded
  3. 1927–1937 — Chinese Civil War (Phase 1)
  4. 1937–1945 — Second Sino-Japanese War
  5. 1946–1949 — Civil War resumes
  6. October 1, 1949 — People’s Republic of China formed

Causes

  • Weak Qing government & foreign influence
  • Rise of nationalism and Sun Yat-sen
  • Conflict between KMT and CCP
  • Impact of Japanese invasion
